Evidence and interpretation

**Results — validation.**

Time-only gross returns were 0.5842–0.6860 bp per evaluable day, against assumed fees of 2.6351–2.6959 bp per day.

All three exit policies had negative net mean returns under both timestamp interpretations at reference execution.

Combined minus time-only was -0.6885 bp per evaluable day under start labels (n=148). The exploratory nominal 95% interval was [-1.7037, -0.0809] bp.

Combined minus time-only was -0.3855 bp per evaluable day under end labels (n=148). The exploratory nominal 95% interval was [-1.2170, 0.1695] bp.

Ambiguous reference episodes: start 0/266, end 1/260.

**Limitations.** These sample estimates condition on common evaluable sessions. Costs and fills are scenarios. All nominal intervals remain exploratory because coverage is not validated. ES has a sparse tail; funded drawdown uses separately identified account paths. Two price paths do not recover actual fills or establish general path robustness.

The final partition was not evaluated in this run.
